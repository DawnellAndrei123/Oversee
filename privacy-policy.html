Oversee Construction Monitoring

Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Oversee Construction Monitoring ("Oversee", "we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use the Oversee construction monitoring application.

Information We Collect

  • Account information, including name, email address, password authentication data, account role, access permissions, and login/session records.
  • Construction workspace data entered by users, including projects, Gantt schedules, SWA records, progress billings, estimates, material price lists, procurement records, accounting records, uploaded plan references, and related notes.
  • Google account information only when you choose to connect Google Drive, including your Google email address and the permissions needed to create and update Oversee files in your Google Drive.
  • Technical data needed to operate and secure the service, such as timestamps, basic request metadata, error logs, and audit records.

How We Use Information

  • To create and manage user accounts, access permissions, owner/member roles, and subscriptions.
  • To provide construction monitoring features such as Gantt charts, SWA, estimates, procurement, accounting, dashboards, and project reporting.
  • To save and sync workspace data between authorized users of the same owner workspace.
  • To create or update an Oversee Google Drive folder and Google Sheet when the account owner connects Google Drive sync.
  • To protect the application, investigate errors or abuse, maintain audit logs, and comply with applicable legal obligations.

Google Drive and Google Sheets Data

If you connect Google Drive, Oversee uses Google OAuth to request permission to create and update files for Oversee's user-facing storage and export features. Oversee may create a folder named "Oversee Construction Data" and a spreadsheet named "Oversee Data - your-email@example.com" in your Google Drive.

Oversee uses Google Drive and Google Sheets access only to provide or improve visible app features that you use, such as saving projects, estimates, SWA, procurement, accounting, and related workspace data into your Google Drive spreadsheet. We do not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or transfer it to advertising platforms, data brokers, or information resellers.

Oversee's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

How Data Is Stored

  • Account and administrative records are stored in the backend database used by Oversee.
  • Workspace data may be stored in backend storage and/or synced to the account owner's connected Google Drive spreadsheet, depending on the active app configuration and sync status.
  • Google refresh tokens, when used, are stored server-side and encrypted or otherwise protected so the app can keep the owner's Oversee spreadsheet updated after authorization.

How We Share Information

We do not sell personal information. We may share or disclose information only when needed to operate the app, provide requested features, support authorized users in the same workspace, protect security, comply with law, or respond to a user's support request.

User Choices and Controls

  • You can disconnect Google Drive sync from the Account area in Oversee.
  • You can delete Oversee-created Google Drive files directly from your Google Drive.
  • Workspace owners can manage member access to Engineering, Procurement, and Accounting views.
  • You may request account or workspace data changes through the support contact for Oversee.

Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards designed to protect account credentials, access tokens, workspace records, and synced data. No online service can guarantee absolute security, so users should keep their own account passwords and Google accounts secure.

Children

Oversee is intended for business and construction project use. It is not directed to children under 13 years old.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the app changes. Material changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the Oversee administrator or use: privacy@overseeconstructions.com.

This policy is a practical template for the current Oversee app. Review it with qualified legal counsel before using Oversee for production customers in jurisdictions with specific privacy requirements.
